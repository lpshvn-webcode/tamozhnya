# Промты для генерации изображений

Пока реальных фото нет. Сгенерированные картинки кладите в `assets/` под теми же именами — страница подхватит их сама. Лучше формат WebP, ширина 1500–1700 px.

**Общий стиль для всех промтов:** photorealistic documentary photography, natural light, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no people's faces in close-up, muted teal and warm gold color grading, 3:2 aspect ratio.

## 1. `assets/warehouse.webp` — крытый склад (блок «Склад», 3:2)
Interior of a clean modern covered customs warehouse in Kazakhstan, about 250 square meters, concrete floor with yellow safety markings, pallets of boxed cargo on steel racks, a forklift in the background, large skylights with soft daylight, wide-angle view, no text.

## 2. `assets/hero.webp` — запасной фон первого экрана (16:9)
Aerial dusk view of a fenced logistics yard near Almaty with a covered warehouse, trucks and cargo containers, snow-capped Trans-Ili Alatau mountains on the horizon, teal-and-gold twilight sky, no text.

## 3. `assets/equipment.webp` — оборудование на досмотре (3:2)
Industrial CNC machine in wooden crate being inspected by a customs officer in a high-visibility vest with a tablet, inside a warehouse inspection zone, forklift nearby, face turned away or out of frame, no text.

## 4. `assets/documents.webp` — разрешительные документы (3:2)
Desk close-up: stack of certificates and declarations with stamps, a tablet showing a blank form, a pen, a calculator, a cargo invoice, soft window light, shallow depth of field, all text blurred and unreadable.

## 5. `assets/auto.webp` — авто и спецтехника (3:2)
Row of newly imported cars and a yellow excavator parked on a clean fenced storage yard at golden hour, mountains in the distance, no license plates readable, no brand logos, no text.

## 6. (по желанию) `assets/team.webp` — команда, 4:3
Three customs brokers in business-casual clothes reviewing documents at a table in a bright office, candid documentary style, natural poses.

Когда появятся реальные фото склада, замените ими файлы 1, 3 и 5: реальные снимки работают лучше сгенерированных.
